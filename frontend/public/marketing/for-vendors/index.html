Good to know
before you join.

Who signs up first?

The delivery company signs up first. It adds its vendors, riders and office teammates, then shares a separate invite code or link with each person. Vendors and riders join the company’s account.

Can one vendor see another vendor’s sales?

No. Every vendor has a separate login and sees only their own products, orders, deliveries and money. Riders see their own drops. The company controls its workspace.

Is a delivered order also a verified payment?

No. Stock and money are tracked separately. A delivered order reduces stock left, but its payment stays under Payment to verify until the office checks that the money landed. Bank and POS auto-verification are not currently available.
