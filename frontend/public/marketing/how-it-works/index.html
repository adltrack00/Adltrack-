How Ada works

From “stock is here”
to “day is done”.

One connected journey for the office, your vendors and your riders. Here’s what a day on Ada looks like.

Try a sample day Meet Ada
01

Stock arrives. Everyone sees it.

The office records the vendor’s products, quantity and exact unit price. The vendor sees their stock immediately.

40 GlowSkin serums × ₦12,500 each
02

The orders find their shape.

Vendors submit orders through the portal or on WhatsApp. Ada reads the details and sets incomplete orders aside to fix. No physical address means no dispatch.

Missing phone? Flag it. Unknown product? Check it.
03

Ada drafts. You decide.

Ask Ada to dispatch. It matches stock and open orders, groups rider batches by area and daily load, totals the amounts to collect and drafts messages. You review and approve.

Draft → review → Approve & send
04

Riders accept and collect.

Each rider gets a batch offer in the app or on WhatsApp. They accept, collect the stock at the office and start their drops. Turned-down batches return to the office’s attention.

Clear stops, route and pay, before the rider accepts.
05

The doorstep becomes a record.

The rider captures the photo, recipient, amount and payment method. The time is stamped. The office and relevant vendor see the same delivered record.

Photo + recipient + time + cash, transfer or POS
06

Stock moves. Money gets checked.

Delivered units leave the left count. The rider hands in cash, the office verifies payments and records each vendor’s payout. A delivery and a verified payment are separate events.

Received − delivered = left. Every naira has its own record.
07

Tomorrow, you can look back.

Open the calendar and choose the day. Review the drops, photos, stock, money and chats. Each role sees only their own part.

The day ends. Its record stays.
