Questions & answers

Good questions.
Straight answers.

From your first company account to the final cash hand-in. The things you should know before you get started.

Who signs up first?

The delivery company signs up first. It adds its vendors, riders and office teammates, then shares a separate invite code or link with each person. Vendors and riders join the company’s account.

Do riders and vendors need to install an app?

No. They can use Ada in a phone browser or work with Ada on WhatsApp when the company connects its WhatsApp Business number. Add Ada to the home screen for a full-screen experience. Android and iPhone store apps are on the way.

Does Ada dispatch without asking me?

No. Ada reads orders, checks information and drafts the stock changes, rider batches and messages. A person reviews the plan and taps Approve & send before those changes are saved.

What counts as proof of delivery?

The rider captures a doorstep photo, the recipient, the exact amount collected and whether it was cash, transfer or POS. Ada adds the timestamp and makes the record visible to the office and the relevant vendor.

Can one vendor see another vendor’s sales?

No. Every vendor has a separate login and sees only their own products, orders, deliveries and money. Riders see their own drops. The company controls its workspace.

What happens when a delivery fails?

The rider records the problem. The drop is marked as not delivered and its units return to the warehouse count. The office can follow up and plan another delivery.

Is a delivered order also a verified payment?

No. Stock and money are tracked separately. A delivered order reduces stock left, but its payment stays under Payment to verify until the office checks that the money landed. Bank and POS auto-verification are not currently available.

How does WhatsApp work?

The company connects an official WhatsApp Business number through Meta Cloud API or Twilio. Ada sends rider batches and vendor updates and reads supported replies. Office staff can step in on any chat. Before connecting a number, the app includes a test-line simulator.

How long does setup take?

The company’s initial setup is designed to take about five minutes. Add your company details and areas, then invite riders and vendors. Connecting a WhatsApp Business number is a separate setup step.

How much does Ada cost?

Pricing has not been finalised. We’ll share clear plans when they’re ready. Get started free to explore the product, or plan a walkthrough around your operation.
