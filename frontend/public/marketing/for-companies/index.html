Good to know
before you join.

Who signs up first?

The delivery company signs up first. It adds its vendors, riders and office teammates, then shares a separate invite code or link with each person. Vendors and riders join the company’s account.

Does Ada dispatch without asking me?

No. Ada reads orders, checks information and drafts the stock changes, rider batches and messages. A person reviews the plan and taps Approve & send before those changes are saved.

How does WhatsApp work?

The company connects an official WhatsApp Business number through Meta Cloud API or Twilio. Ada sends rider batches and vendor updates and reads supported replies. Office staff can step in on any chat. Before connecting a number, the app includes a test-line simulator.
