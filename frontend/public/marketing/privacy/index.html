This is the Ada marketing website and an illustrative product demo.

Sample data, not real deliveries

The interactive demo uses fictional orders, riders, vendors and amounts. It is not connected to a live company workspace. Demo progress is saved in this browser’s local storage so that company, vendor and rider views can show the same sample drop. Reset demo clears that sample progress.

Photos in the demo

A photo you choose in the sample rider flow is processed in your browser to preview the proof step. It is not uploaded to a server or saved with your account. Use the sample photo if you prefer. The website’s campaign imagery is AI-generated and illustrative; it is not a photograph of a customer, employee or completed delivery.

Your demo brief

The Book a demo page prepares text in your browser. It does not send an email or book a meeting. You can download the brief and decide whether and where to share it. Form details are not sent by this site.

Company accounts

Real company sign-in, signup and invitations belong to the production Ada application. This site links directly to the production application for sign-in, company signup and invitations. This preview does not collect passwords or create operational accounts.

The real product

In the Ada product, the company, each vendor and each rider have separate access. Vendors see their own stock, deliveries and money; riders see their own drops. A published product privacy policy and service terms should be reviewed before any public account launch.
